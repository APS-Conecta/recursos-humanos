**Which law fills the gaps.** Ley 18.883 applies to APS staff for anything Ley 19.378 does not expressly regulate (L19378 art. 4 inc. 1; D1889 art. 4). Ley 18.834 is not the supplementary law for APS. CGR applies the supplementary law only where Ley 19.378 is silent (CGR E15824N25, 2025). One exception: for *profesionales funcionarios* covered by Ley 15.076, the supplementary law on concursos, jornada, feriados and permisos is Ley 15.076, "en cuanto sean conciliables" (L19378 art. 4 inc. 3).

## 1. Feriado legal

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Staff with **more than one year of service** | L19378 art. 18 inc. 1; D1889 art. 4 bis |
| Days per calendar year | **15 días hábiles** with under 15 years of service; **20** with 15 to under 20 years; **25** with 20 or more years | L19378 art. 18 inc. 2; D1889 art. 4 bis inc. 1 |
| Saturdays | Not días hábiles. Sundays and holidays are also excluded under CGR jurisprudence. | L19378 art. 18 inc. 5; CGR 011162N15 |
| Shift staff (turnos) | A continuous feriado counts only Monday to Friday. A fractioned feriado must cover every day the person is rostered to work, since "todos los días del año pasan a ser hábiles" for them. | CGR 011162N15 (2015, APS) |
| Extreme zones | **+5 días hábiles** for staff who work and live in regions I, II, XII or XV, or in the provinces of Palena or Chiloé. D1889 grants it only when the feriado is taken in another region or abroad, proven with tickets or a sworn declaration within the 5 days before. Aysén (XI) is not listed in L19378 art. 18, although L18883 art. 105 includes it. | L19378 art. 18 inc. 3; D1889 art. 4 bis inc. 2–3 |
| Juan Fernández | The feriado is extended by the days needed to travel to the mainland and back | L19378 art. 18 inc. 4; D1889 art. 4 bis |
| Counting years of service | Counts years worked in the **public sector under any legal status**, in municipal establishments, in private APS corporations, and in PEM, POJH and PRORH programmes in the health sector, accredited with certificates. Unlike L18883 art. 102, private-sector employment does not count. A private body counts only if it performs APS functions (CGR 061167N06). | L19378 art. 18 inc. 5; D1889 art. 4 bis inc. 4 |
| First year | Prior public-sector time counts toward the first year. Periods under reemplazo and plazo-fijo contracts are added up even with gaps between them; the year need not be continuous. | CGR 000313N16 (2016, APS), applying 62.274/2009 |
| Request and approval | The employee requests a date. The feriado "en ningún caso podrá ser denegado discrecionalmente". The **Director** may bring it forward or postpone it when the establishment needs it, but only within the same year. | L19378 art. 18 inc. 6–7 |
| Accumulation | Only when the employee **expressly asks** to combine it with the next year's feriado. **At most two consecutive periods.** Feriado not used in its year is otherwise lost. | L19378 art. 18 inc. 7; CGR 030269N19 |
| Accumulation when blocked by licencia or maternity | Allowed (CGR 030269N19, 2019, reconsidering earlier rulings), but the two-period cap still applies (CGR E179972N25, 2025, under Ley 18.834). | as cited |
| Fractioning | Allowed, but **one fraction must be at least 10 days**. Exceptions: half days are allowed while on postnatal parental leave at half time (CGR 002751N20, an APS case) and while on a partial medical licence (CGR E368448N23). A remainder under 10 days is allowed when a licencia shortened the feriado (CGR D110N26, 2026, under Ley 18.883). | L19378 art. 18 inc. 8 |
| Medical leave during feriado | The feriado is **suspended** if the employee falls ill or has an accident that entitles them to a licencia médica (CGR E378921N23, 2023, for Ley 18.834 and 18.883 staff). It reaches APS staff through the supplementary rule; no APS-specific ruling was found. | CGR E378921N23 |
| Part-time staff | Same number of days as full-time staff. The count depends on seniority, not on weekly hours, and Saturdays, Sundays and holidays are non-working days. | DT5734 (2015) |
| Feriado progresivo | The 15/20/25 scale in art. 18 is itself what CGR calls the "feriado progresivo"; there is no separate progressive bonus. | L19378 art. 18; CGR 061167N06 |
| Cash-out | Feriado can only be used while the person is still an employee | CGR 068604N09 (APS) |

## 2. Permisos administrativos (días administrativos)

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quantity | Up to **6 días hábiles per calendar year**, with pay, for personal reasons | L19378 art. 17 inc. 1 |
| Fractioning | By **days or half days** | L19378 art. 17 inc. 1 |
| Approval | **Granted or denied by the Director del establecimiento**, according to the needs of the service, so they may be refused | L19378 art. 17 inc. 1; CGR 054952N04 |
| Carry-over | **None. Unused days are lost** ("los días no utilizados se pierden"), and they cannot be paid out. | CGR E155421N21 (2021, under Ley 18.883 art. 108); CGR 005192N18 |
| Part-time (22 h, 33 h, etc.) | **The full 6 days, not prorated.** A "día" means that employee's daily jornada, whatever its length. | CGR 054952N04 (2004, APS); DT5734 |
| Reemplazo staff | Entitled, but only within the term of the reemplazo contract | CGR 091025N15 (APS) |
| Staff hired mid-year | **UNCONFIRMED.** The law says "hasta seis días hábiles en el año calendario" and contains no proportionality clause. No dictamen was found either way. | L19378 art. 17 |
| Interaction with CT art. 199 bis | Time used under that leave (a seriously ill child, or a terminal spouse or parent) must first be offset against días administrativos, then against the next feriado, the next year's días administrativos, or overtime | CT art. 199 bis inc. 5 |

## 5. Calificaciones (L19378 arts. 44 and 46; D1889 arts. 58–71)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who is rated | Every member of the dotación, every year, if they worked **at least 6 months** in the period (continuous or not). Those not rated keep their previous rating. The establishment director is rated by their hierarchical superior. Commission members are rated by the commission, excluding the person concerned. | D1889 arts. 33, 63; L19378 art. 44 inc. 2 |
| Period | **1 September to 31 August.** The process runs from **1 September and must end by 31 December**. | D1889 arts. 63–64 |
| Lists | **Lista 1 (Distinción), 2 (Buena), 3 (Condicional), 4 (Eliminación).** **No national point ranges exist:** each administering entity's rating regulation sets them. | D1889 art. 60; L19378 art. 30 bis |
| Factors and scale | Total 100 points: **Competencia up to 40**, **Conducta funcionaria up to 30**, **Desempeño en equipos de trabajo up to 30**. The team factor scores only when team goals are met in whole or in part, in proportion to how far they are met, with at least 3 levels of achievement. If no team goals were set, its 30 points are shared proportionally between the other two factors. | D1889 arts. 68, 69, 71 |
| Subfactors | Set by the entity for each category, together with their weights, the list ranges, and the teams and their goals. The factor descriptions list example aspects: Competencia covers efficiency, user service, knowledge, interest, initiative and responsibility; Conducta covers collaboration, punctuality, attendance, behaviour and compliance. | D1889 arts. 68, 70 |
| Goals at the start | Written individual and team goals or commitments. The employee is told who their jefe directo is and which instruments will be used. | D1889 art. 59 |
| Precalificación | By the **jefe directo**, as a written report in **concepts, not points**. **At least two** per period, and they must take account of anotaciones de mérito and demérito. The commission must consider it but is not bound by it (CGR 056260N16). | D1889 arts. 59, 67 |
| Comisión de Calificación | One health professional from the entity, appointed by the head of the entity, who **presides**; the **director** of the employee's establishment, or someone the head appoints; and **two staff of the same category** as the rated employee, **elected by vote** of the staff being rated. Categories may be merged when staff are too few. Quorum is a majority of members; the president breaks ties. Decisions must be reasoned and recorded in minutes, and members cannot delegate their role. | L19378 art. 44; D1889 arts. 61–62 |
| Notification | Delivering a certified copy of the commission's agreement to the employee. **No deadline is set** in Ley 19.378 or D1889. Whether the 5-day rules in L18883 art. 45 apply to APS: **UNCONFIRMED**. | D1889 art. 65 |
| Apelación | To the **Alcalde**, within **10 días hábiles** of notification. Must be decided within **15 días hábiles**. The Alcalde may keep or **raise** the score, not lower it. The decision must be reasoned (CGR 056307N16). | L19378 art. 46; D1889 art. 65 |
| Reclamo to CGR | Within **10 días hábiles** under L18883 arts. 47 and 156, applied to APS. CGR reviews only legality and procedure, not the merits. | CGR 056307N16, 056260N16 |
| Hoja de vida and anotaciones | D1889 requires the precalificación to weigh the anotaciones de mérito and demérito "que constan en la hoja funcionaria", and records rating scores and sumarios in the Hoja de Carrera (art. 29). Anotaciones are defined and handled in L18883 arts. 37–41: made by the personnel unit at the written request of the jefe directo; the employee may ask for merit notes or for a demérito to be removed; they cover only the period being rated. Applying these 18.883 articles to APS is **UNCONFIRMED** (no specific ruling). Sanctions and deméritos count only in the period they belong to (D1889 art. 59). | as cited |
| Consequences | **Lista 4**, or **Lista 3 for two consecutive periods or three in total**, ends the employment. Lista 1 or 2 **and** within the best-rated **35%** of the category in the establishment earns the **asignación anual de mérito**: top 11% up to 35% of the national minimum base salary (sueldo base mínimo nacional), next 11% up to 20%, last 13% up to 10%. Paid in March, June, September and December. Tie-breaks follow D1889 art. 35. | L19378 arts. 48 f), 30 bis; D2296 art. 1 f); D1889 arts. 32–36 |

## 6. Carrera funcionaria (L19378 arts. 5–9, 37–42; D1889 arts. 8–12, 18–57)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Categories | **A**: médicos, farmacéuticos, químico-farmacéuticos, bioquímicos and cirujano-dentistas. **B**: other professionals, with a degree of at least 8 semesters. **C**: técnico de nivel superior. **D**: técnico de salud (enseñanza media plus a 1,500-hour course). **E**: administrativo (enseñanza media). **F**: auxiliar (enseñanza básica). ## UNCONFIRMED items

1. Whether staff hired mid-year get all 6 días administrativos or a proportional share: the law is silent and no dictamen was found.
2. Whether L18883 art. 107 (the alcalde grants or denies discretionally) governs APS unpaid leave.
3. Whether the 5-day birth leave (CT art. 195) is counted in días hábiles or corridos.
4. Whether the 5-day training leave (L19378 art. 43) is counted in días hábiles or corridos.
5. Notification deadline for ratings in APS, and whether L18883 art. 45 applies.
6. Whether L18883 arts. 37–41 (hoja de vida and anotaciones) apply to APS: D1889 refers to anotaciones but does not define them.
7. Suspension of feriado by licencia médica, and the CT art. 66 bereavement leave, reach APS only through the supplementary rule; no APS-specific dictamen was found.
8. How Ley 15.076 rules on feriado and permisos for category A professionals interact with L19378 arts. 17 and 18: the only dictamen found (CGR 072396N16) concerns a CRS, not municipal APS.
9. Whether the Aysén +5 days applies: L19378 art. 18 omits region XI, but L18883 art. 105 includes it.
10. National point ranges for the four lists: none exist; each entity's regulation sets them.
