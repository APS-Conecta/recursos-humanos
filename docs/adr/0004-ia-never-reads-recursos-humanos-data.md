# 4. «IA» never reads «Recursos Humanos» data

Accepted, 2026-11-19.

«IA» reads what a person can open (ia ADR-0001), and the Record sheet folder is open to the Overview groups. Personnel
records, Sick leave dates and ratings must stay out of the Model's reach, so the app's folder and files are kept out of
every «IA» source, and the app exposes nothing to the suite's AI layer. On that basis the owner ships the app without
waiting for the Ley 21.719 privacy review that «IA» and the Buscador wait for.

## Consequences

- A future wish such as «you have 2 days left» in a Bulletin needs a new decision and the privacy review first.
