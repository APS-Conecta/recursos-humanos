# 5. Two establishment rules depart from Ley 19.378, as named Rule versions

Accepted, 2026-11-19 (owner's ruling, against the letter of the law).

- A Jefatura may reject Annual leave like any other Request, always with a written reason. Ley 19.378 art. 18 says it
  "en ningún caso podrá ser denegado discrecionalmente"; only the Director may move it within the year.
- A Sick leave that falls inside a Registered Annual leave does not suspend it: no days are given back. Contraloría
  rulings (e.g. E378921N23) say the feriado is suspended.

Both are named Rule versions marked «regla del establecimiento» with their own dates, so a Contraloría ruling or a new
law switches the app back to the law's reading with a new Rule version and no code change (ADR-0002). Shift staff
counting Monday to Friday is a third establishment rule of the same kind (CGR 11.162/2015 counts every rostered day).
